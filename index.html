Context & vision

Built to become the continental reference for refereeing intelligence.

ARIS (Africa Referee Intelligence System) is a standalone platform developed by Prestige Solutions of Afrika, entirely dedicated to the refereeing ecosystem: performance evaluation, intelligent designation of referee teams, and career management.

Who it serves

Federations, leagues, CAF, and its regional zones — UNIFFAC, UFOA, COSAFA, CECAFA, UNAF — plus major competitions: AFCON, CHAN, and CAF interclub tournaments.

Where it starts

No development has been engaged on ARIS to date — this platform previews the functional scope defined in the ARIS Cahier des Charges v2.0, July 2026, not a shipped product.

Continental reach

Five CAF zones targeted from day one, with a data model designed to scale to a pan-African digital referee passport.

Integration model

Two products, one API — never a dependency.

ARIS and AfriScout stay fully distinct products, connected only through a two-way API data exchange, governed by a private data-sharing agreement.

AfriScout → ARIS

What ARIS consumes:

  • • Position & context of already-tagged disciplinary events (fouls, cards) for the Decision Engine and Heat Maps.
  • • Match tempo & pressure data to contextualize the Pressure Index.
  • • Match result & metadata for RMCS calculation.

Access: private API by default — a public aggregated, non-nominative feed may be considered separately.

ARIS → AfriScout

What ARIS can provide (value-add, not a dependency):

  • • A “Match refereeing quality” block, embeddable in an AfriScout match report.
  • • The timestamped VAR / refereeing decision log, to complete AfriScout report timelines.
  • • The designated referee team's profile, for match preview sheets.

AfriScout does not cover refereeing — this is a one-directional value offer from ARIS.

Structural advantage

ARIS doesn't start from zero — AfriScout already exists.

No identified competitor — Western assignment platforms, TrackSwift, or CAF initiatives — has a sister scouting & video-tagging platform already operational. That's ARIS's most concrete structural edge.

Automated video decision review (no VAR hardware)

Lever: Reuses the existing AfriScout video tagging pipeline — same match footage, same trained taggers.

Value: Post-hoc decision support at marginal cost, where classic VAR needs dedicated cameras & operators — out of reach for most African leagues.

Pressure-adjusted decision score

Lever: Combines AfriScout's existing metrics (PPDA, momentum, tempo, 15-min intensity) with refereeing decisions.

Value: No identified competitor links match tactical context to decision quality — a unique differentiator.

Player-referee friction risk index

Lever: Cross-references AfriScout's player disciplinary history with the assigned referee's profile.

Value: Feeds RMCS with a predictive signal neither TrackSwift nor Arbiter can produce without a player database.

Shared tagger workforce

Lever: Taggers already trained for AfriScout can be cross-trained to capture referee events (position, whistles, cards).

Value: Saves the time and cost of recruiting/training a dedicated ARIS data-collection team.

Instant coverage of already-tagged matches

Lever: Every match already covered by AfriScout becomes automatically available for refereeing analysis.

Value: Faster launch than any competitor who'd need to negotiate footage access match by match.

Combined Match + Refereeing report

Lever: One PDF dossier presents both tactical analysis (AfriScout) and refereeing quality (ARIS).

Value: A sellable product to leagues and media that no identified competitor unifies today.

Single club/federation identifier

Lever: A client of both platforms has one account and one shared match calendar.

Value: Reduces adoption friction for clients running both products in parallel.
